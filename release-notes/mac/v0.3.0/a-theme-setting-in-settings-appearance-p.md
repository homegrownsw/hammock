A Theme setting in Settings ▸ Appearance picks your terminal, window and agent view colours and the active pane border together. Choose Hammock's own tints or Catppuccin, Tokyo Night, Gruvbox, Nord, Rosé Pine or Solarized, or Custom… to set each yourself. If you'd chosen your own colours they carry over as Custom; otherwise you start on the Hammock theme. It's also in View ▸ Theme, the command palette and `hmk theme` (`hmk theme nord`), and your paired iPhone follows it.
