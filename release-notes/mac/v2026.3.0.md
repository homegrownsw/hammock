# Mac 2026.3.0

## New

- Dictate to your agents: click the microphone in the agent view (or press ⇧⌘M) and your words appear as you speak. Turn the button off in Settings ▸ Agent View.
- Choose where links you click open: Settings ▸ General ▸ Links ▸ Clicked links open in Default Browser, a Hammock Pane beside it, or a Hammock Tab.
- Editor and diff panes can have a font of their own, in Settings ▸ Editor.
- Hammock now comes with five terminal fonts: Obel Mono, JetBrains Mono, Fira Code, Cascadia Code and Hack, in Settings ▸ Terminal. Nerd Font icons in prompts show whatever font you pick, with no Nerd Font installed.
- Horizontal Tabs now look nicer and keep your selected tab visible.
- In the agent view, a reply that arrives while you watch fades in from the top, and a long one stops at its first line instead of scrolling past it to the end.
- In the agent view, long lines in a tool's command and output now wrap, so you can read them without scrolling sideways.
- Programs that say what they're doing with the Program Status Protocol (OSC 7501) now show it in the sidebar the way agents do. They have their own Needs You notifications, which you can turn on and off in Settings ▸ Notifications.
- Setup now helps you give Hammock Full Disk Access so programs in your panes can reach your files and other apps' data.
- Sleeping agents: turn on Settings' new option to put idle agents to sleep after 3 to 48 hours and their processes end to free memory.
- Split panes have a thin outline, and the active pane's border sits on the pane's edge instead of over what's in it.

## Fixed

- Settings ▸ Agent View's preview sits under the text and code settings it shows.
- A video you attach in the agent view for an agent on another computer shows up straight away, in the strip and as its #1 in your message, while it's made smaller, so it no longer pops in partway through what you're typing.
- An agent waiting on a question no longer shows as working, and stays in Needs You, when you click into its pane, scroll it or move through its options, then leave without answering.
- Connecting agents during setup: with more than two found, the cards sit two to a row so names and paths aren't cut short, and the button says Connect All rather than Connect Both.
- Hammock's background service no longer crashes when a linked computer drops its connection while it's still connecting.
- hmk ls and the panes agents see now call a log pane a log pane, not a terminal.
- Horizontal Tabs: opening a Group with more tabs than fit shows its selected tab, not the first few.
- Horizontal Tabs: the tab bar no longer stretches up into the toolbar, and panes meet it squarely instead of with rounded corners.
- On a very busy Mac, a new pane no longer fails to open because its terminal took a few seconds to start.
- Show Earlier Messages in the agent view keeps your place, and Latest no longer flashes blank on the way down.
- The agent view no longer goes blank above Working… when the agent starts using tools straight after your message.
- The agent view opens at the end of a long conversation without jumping, and new messages glide in rather than jumping, also after switching back to it.
- ⌘V with an image on the clipboard, like a screenshot, pastes it into Claude Code in a terminal pane, as in other terminals. Files copied in the Finder paste as their paths.

## Internal

- Agent tooling from a retro of recent sessions: `just test` takes a package, `test-quick`, gofmt in `just vet`, `scripts/preflight` before releases, a pre-push hook keeping a release's commit off main, `just land`, a test that every daemon message is placed in each gate, debug captures saying why they failed, and `scripts/frames`.
- Diff and editor panes hold their session with a lease (PaneLease), as the agent view does, instead of releasing it in onDisappear.
- Tests: the membership tests run side by side and the daemon package takes about a third of the time; flaky tests fixed (a mesh check that didn't wait, races in the holder's proof and daemon shutdown, pane holders leaking pseudo-terminals); CI runs the holder package under -race.
