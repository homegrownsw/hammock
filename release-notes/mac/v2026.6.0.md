# Mac 2026.6.0

## New

- Simulator clipboard: send your clipboard to an iOS simulator or Android emulator, or copy its clipboard back, from the pane's header, the Pane menu or ⇧⌘V and ⇧⌘C.
- Menu Bar: an orange count beside Hammock's menu bar icon shows how many agents need you, and choosing one in its menu, or a finished one, opens its tab.
- herdr import: if you use herdr, setup offers to bring its workspaces over as Groups, with their tabs, splits and folders, and resumes their agents' conversations.
- Run Early: run a scheduled task now instead of at its next time, so it doesn’t run twice.

## Fixed

- Screen Sharing: when several of your devices watch this Mac through another of your Macs, the menu bar names each one, and ending one no longer ends them all.
- Notifications to your iPhone are no longer lost when the push relay is briefly busy.
- Simulator panes: pinch now works on the iPhone Duo's inner screen.
- Pane headers: in a narrow pane, buttons that don't fit move into a More menu or step aside, instead of being cut off.
- The menu bar's menu shows Hammock's and the server's version numbers, lined up under the server's status, and Remote Access no longer looks greyed out.
- Attaching to a terminal from another device or window now shows its full scrollback, not just the last few hundred lines.
- Settings and hmk now speak of your phone, iPhone or Android, rather than only an iPhone.
- The dot that goes round a resting agent's icon while its shells run is now bigger and easier to spot.
- The sidebar's usage limits and Mac name no longer sit on top of its tabs straight after setup.

## Internal

- hmkd --dev-pairing (development builds only) lets scripts and agents make pairing codes for a scratch daemon, for building the phone apps.
- The daemon knows Android phones: they count as viewers, and the audit log and Devices name them.
