Change any keyboard shortcut: Settings ▸ Keyboard lists every action, with search that finds them by name, by a typo of it or by the shortcut itself, and you record a new shortcut by pressing it. Shortcuts are keybind lines in ~/.config/fika/config, so you can also edit them there, and the menus, command palette and tooltips show yours.
