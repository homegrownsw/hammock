Open a tab, Group or pane in a window of its own, to share just that on a call: right-click it and choose Open in New Window, or use the Window menu. It's the same tab, Group or pane, not a copy, and closing the window closes nothing. A tab or pane fills its window; a Group's window has only its own tabs. Agents can open one for you too, as can hmk window open.
