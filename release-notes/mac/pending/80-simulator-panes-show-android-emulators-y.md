Simulator panes show Android emulators: your Android Virtual Devices are listed under Android and Android TV, booted with no window (or shown, if one's running). Tap, swipe, pinch, type, rotate and fold them, with Back, Home and Recents in the header; an Android TV has a remote beside it. One running in Android Studio can be restarted in Hammock from its pane. Agents drive them with the same sim tools, and install apps, open links and read logcat.
