date: 2026-11-10
Hammock's screen sharing, browser and simulator helpers now take instructions only from Hammock itself. Another program on your Mac, such as an agent in a pane, can no longer start a screen sharing helper of its own, or slip into the one Hammock started, to look at your screen or click and type for you: the helper now serves only the Hammock that started it, which proves itself with a secret only it was given.
