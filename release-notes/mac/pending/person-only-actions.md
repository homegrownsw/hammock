What only you can do in Hammock (answering an agent's request, pairing a phone, sharing, linking computers, remote access, SSH setup, saving a schedule) is now done directly only in the Hammock app. Asked from anywhere else, `hmk` in a pane's shell or a terminal and the TUI included, it asks you first: allow it once in Needs You on your Mac or from the notification on your phone, and `hmk` waits for your answer. Answering requests happens only in the app or on your phone. Until anything could ask you (no phone paired, no computer linked, the app not open, as on a new Linux server), a terminal of yours still does these directly, and the first phone or computer paired is told so. A program left running in the background (and `hmk` run from launchd, cron or a terminal multiplexer) is treated like an agent: it asks before typing into, closing or driving a pane it didn't open. Pane holders also take only Hammock's own daemon.
