Pairing a phone, sharing, linking computers, turning on remote access, setting up SSH and saving a scheduled task are now done directly only in the Hammock app. Asked for anywhere else (`hmk` in a terminal or a pane, or the TUI), Hammock asks you first, in Needs You on your Mac or in a notification on your phone, and `hmk` waits for your answer. The request says what it would do and where it came from, including when something other than you typed into that pane just before. Answering agents' requests happens only in the app or on your phone. On a new Mac or server with nothing paired yet, the first pairing from a terminal goes ahead, and Hammock tells you about it afterwards.
