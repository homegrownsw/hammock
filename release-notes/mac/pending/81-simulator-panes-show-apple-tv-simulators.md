Simulator panes show Apple TV simulators, with a Siri Remote beside the screen to click, hold and swipe (Pane ▸ Show Remote, ⌃⌘R). With the pane focused, the arrow keys, Return, Escape and Space are the remote's clickpad, Select, Back and Play/Pause. Agents drive it with the new sim_remote tool, and hmk sim remote.
