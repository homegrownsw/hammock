Sleeping agents: turn on Settings' new option to put idle agents to sleep after 3 to 48 hours (or choose Put Agent to Sleep), and their processes end to free memory. The pane keeps its screen, dimmed under a Wake button: click it (or type, or send a message) and the session resumes where it left off, with the options it was started with. After a restart, agents now resume with those options too.
