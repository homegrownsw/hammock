Choose where links you click open: Settings ▸ General ▸ Links ▸ Clicked links open in Default Browser, a Hammock Pane beside it, or a Hammock Tab. It applies to links you ⌘-click in a terminal and click in the agent view; ⌘⇧-click opens the other way, and a link's right-click menu offers all three.
