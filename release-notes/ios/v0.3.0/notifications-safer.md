Answering from a notification (Allow, Deny, Let View, Decline) now needs your iPhone unlocked, and with Require Face ID on it opens Hammock to ask first. Notifications take everything they show, their buttons included, only from what your Mac encrypted, and old ones replayed later show nothing. The keys for them are no longer in iPhone backups: a restored iPhone makes new ones when it next connects to each Mac.
