# iOS 0.4.0

## New

- The Hammock Key's approval sheet on iPhone is clearer: it opens at full height, shows the Group and tab asking as the Mac does, and its Approve button shows Touch ID on phones with it.
- Agents can be approved for 5 minutes with the Hammock Key: tick the box on the Mac's prompt, or choose Approve for 5 Minutes on your iPhone, so a script's few commits or a burst of SSH commands asks once. An agent's window is its own: your programs in that pane still ask.
- Diff panes on your iPhone: review the changes an agent's made, comment on a line or a file, send your comments to it and see its replies.

## Fixed

- Notifications about an agent, a permission, someone asking to join or a Hammock Key request now leave Notification Center once they're dealt with, wherever you dealt with them, instead of piling up (at the latest when you next open Hammock).
- Start an Agent: a gap between the chosen Group's picture or emoji and its name.

## Internal

- The demo's shell answers a few common commands (git status, git push, ls, npm test), and a Debug-only director (HMK_DEBUG_TAKE) plays scripted steps in the demo, for the App Store's videos.
- The demo's terminals take the size the phone asks for, and its shell shows a fuller session; HMK_DEBUG_SCREENSHOT (Debug) hides the demo's bar and says when the screen has settled, for the App Store screenshots.
