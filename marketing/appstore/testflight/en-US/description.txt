Hammock keeps your terminals and coding agents running on your Mac. Hammock for iPhone shows you what your agents are doing, lets you answer them when they need you (allow a command, answer a question, send a message), and notifies you the moment one does. You can also type into your Mac's terminals, see its Groups and panes, and start new agents.

It pairs directly with Hammock on your own Mac by scanning a code: there's no account. You'll need Hammock for Mac, free at homegrownsw.com/hammock. Without one, tap "See a demo" on the first screen to look around.
