Support

Hammock is free and open source, made by Homegrown Software. Here's how to get help.

Report a bug or ask for something Open an issue on GitHub. Say what you did, what you expected and what happened, and which versions of Hammock and macOS (or iOS) you're on. Email us No GitHub account, or something you'd rather not post in public? Write to hammock@homegrownsw.com. Report a security problem Privately, through GitHub's vulnerability reporting, never in a public issue.

How security reports are handled: the security policy.

Getting started

  • Hammock for Mac needs macOS 26 or later. Download it, open the disk image and drag Hammock to Applications. It updates itself.
  • Hammock for iPhone pairs with Hammock on your Mac. On the Mac, open Hammock ▸ Settings ▸ Devices, turn on remote access and choose Show Pairing Code, then scan it with the iPhone app. No Mac to hand? Tap "See a demo" on the iPhone app's first screen.
  • Linux and Unraid: see running Hammock on Linux.

Common questions

My iPhone can't connect to my Mac. The iPhone reaches your Mac over your local network or a VPN such as Tailscale, so both need to be on one of those. On the Mac, check Settings ▸ Devices says remote access is on and lists the network you're using.

I don't get notifications on my iPhone. Allow notifications for Hammock in the iPhone's Settings, and open the app once while it's connected to your Mac, so the Mac learns where to send them.

Do I need an account? No. There's nothing to sign up for: your iPhone pairs with your own Mac. See the privacy policy for what Hammock does with your data.
