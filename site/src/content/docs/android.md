---
title: Using the Android app
description: Follow your agents, answer them, and type in your terminals from an Android phone or tablet.
---

The Android app does what the [iPhone app](/hammock/docs/iphone/) does, in Android's way. To set it up, see
[Installing on Android](/hammock/docs/install/android/).

## Finding your way

The bar along the bottom (along the side on a tablet or an open foldable) has **Sessions**, **Groups**, **Devices**
and **Settings**. Tap a session or tab to open it, and swipe back from the edge of the screen to return. On a tablet,
what you open shows beside the list.

- **Sessions** lists your agents, most pressing first, as on the iPhone. **Allow**, **Deny** and **Open** answer a
  permission request where it is; when what it asks is more than fits, **Review** opens the pane in place of **Allow**.
  Swipe a session left to close it, or touch and hold it for **Allow** and **Deny** when it's asking permission, and its
  pane's actions, such as **Interrupt**.
- **Scheduled Tasks** (**See All** under Coming Up): touch and hold a task, there or in Coming Up, for **Run Now**,
  **Skip Next Run**, **Pause** and the rest; swipe one left in the list to pause or resume it.
- **Groups** shows each Group and its tabs. Touch and hold a tab for its actions; swipe it left to close it. In
  **Edit**, drag a tab by its handle to move it.
- An agent's pane shows its conversation or its terminal: choose in its ⋮ menu, which also has the tab's actions
  (**Rename…**, **Put Agent to Sleep**, **Close Tab** and the rest).
- A tab with several panes has a strip along the bottom with a pill for each, as on the iPhone: tap one, or swipe
  sideways across the pane, to switch; touch and hold a pill for more. The button at the strip's left opens Exposé,
  the tab laid out as on the Mac.
- While the phone is connecting to your computer, or has lost it, a banner under each screen's title says so, and
  after a few seconds that it can't reach it, with how long ago what's shown was last updated. It keeps trying;
  **Try Again** tries now, and so does pulling down on Sessions or Groups.

## Terminals and the agent view

Tap a terminal for the keyboard, with **esc**, **tab**, **ctrl** and **alt** (which apply to the next key), the
arrows and delete above it; swipe them for symbols such as `|` and `~`, Home and End. Drag to scroll
back, and pinch to change the font size for every terminal. Touch and hold to select a word, keep dragging or move its handles to select more, then **Copy**;
**Select all** selects the screen, and **Paste** pastes. **Copy Screen** is in the pane's ⋮ menu. While the phone can't
reach your computer, keys do nothing and **Offline** shows above the keyboard: tap it to try again. When a
terminal's program fails, a bar along its bottom has **Run Again** (or **Resume**, for an agent) and **Close**. In the agent
view, type in the box at the bottom and tap the arrow to send; while the agent works, the same button stops it. Type
`/` for the agent's commands, or `@` for a file in the Group's folder, and tap one to put it in. Tap the context gauge
to compact or clear the conversation. **+** attaches pictures, and the microphone dictates on the phone itself, where
Android has the language installed. With a hardware keyboard, ↑ in the empty box brings back what you sent before;
on a prompt, press an option's number to answer, ← and → to move between questions, and Esc for the terminal.

Browser and simulator panes show live, and you can tap, scroll and type in them. A Mac with screen sharing on appears
in **Devices** under **Show Screen**: see [Screen sharing](/hammock/docs/screen-sharing/).

## Notifications and the Hammock Key

Notifications come while you're away from your Mac, each in its channel (Needs You, Finished, Errors, Hammock Key,
Join Requests), and carry the count the Sessions tab shows: agents that need your input, and permission, join and
Hammock Key requests. **Allow** and **Deny** on a permission request, and **Let View**
on a join request, answer it once you've unlocked the phone; with **Require Unlock** on, they open Hammock and answer
once you've unlocked it. A request to sign with your Mac's
[Hammock Key](/hammock/docs/remote/#the-hammock-key) opens Hammock, where **Approve** asks for your fingerprint, face
or screen lock.

## Settings

The app's settings are its own: the theme (by default your Mac's; **Wallpaper Colours** takes your phone's), the
agent view's text and code, the terminal's and editors' fonts and spacing, attachments, dictation, the warning before
closing an agent at work, usage limits, notifications, and **Require Unlock**, which locks Hammock when you come back to
it and hides it in Recents.
