This build:
[NEW] The Hammock Key's approval sheet on iPhone is clearer: it opens at full height, shows the Group and tab asking as the Mac does, and its Approve button shows Touch ID on phones with it.
[NEW] Agents can be approved for 5 minutes with the Hammock Key: tick the box on the Mac's prompt, or choose Approve for 5 Minutes on your iPhone, so a script's few commits or a burst of SSH commands asks once. An agent's window is its own: your programs in that pane still ask.
[NEW] Diff panes on your iPhone: review the changes an agent's made, comment on a line or a file, send your comments to it and see its replies.
[FIXED] Notifications about an agent, a permission, someone asking to join or a Hammock Key request now leave Notification Center once they're dealt with, wherever you dealt with them, instead of piling up (at the latest when you next open Hammock).
[FIXED] Start an Agent: a gap between the chosen Group's picture or emoji and its name.
