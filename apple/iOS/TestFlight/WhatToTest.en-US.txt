This build

New
• Dictate to your agents: tap the microphone beside Send in a session and your words appear as you speak, on your iPhone. Turn the button off in Settings.
• A reply that arrives while you watch fades in from the top, and a long one stops at its first line instead of scrolling past it to the end.
• Editors and diffs can have a font of their own, in Settings ▸ Editors and Diffs.
• In the agent view, long lines in a tool's command and output now wrap, so you can read them without swiping sideways.
• Pick a terminal font from Obel Mono, JetBrains Mono, Fira Code, Cascadia Code and Hack in Settings. Icons in prompts from Nerd Fonts now show in any font, and editor and diff screens use your terminal font.
• Programs on your Mac that say what they're doing, like a long build or a deploy script, now show it the way agents do, including how far along they are. They can notify you too: choose which in Settings ▸ Notify Me When.
• Sleeping agents: when your Mac puts an idle agent to sleep to save memory, it shows a moon in your Groups and sessions, and its screen is dimmed under a Wake Agent button.
• Swipe left on a session in the Sessions tab to close it.

Fixed
• In Settings, the agent view's preview sits right under the text settings it shows.
• A conversation no longer goes blank, all but Working…, when the agent starts using tools straight after your message.
• A video or photo you attach in the agent view shows up straight away, in the strip and as its #1 in your message, while it's prepared, so it no longer pops in partway through what you're typing.
• An agent waiting on a question no longer shows as working, and stays in Needs You, when you tap its pane, scroll it or move through its options, then leave without answering.
• Diff and editor tabs no longer open blank the second time you open them.
• Going back from a conversation no longer flashes No Session Selected as it slides away.
• Opening a long conversation, it shows at its end straight away, not partway up and then jumping down.
• Show Earlier Messages keeps your place, and Latest no longer flashes blank on the way down.
• The conversation moves smoothly with the keyboard, and new messages and tool calls glide into view instead of jumping, also after you've gone back to the list and returned.
