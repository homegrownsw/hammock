This build

New
• A tab with several panes opens straight onto one. Switch with the strip of panes along the bottom, or swipe sideways between them. The button at its left shows the whole tab, laid out as on your Mac, with every pane live.
• Android emulators from your Mac: tap, swipe, pinch and type, with Back, Home and Recents in the bar at the bottom.
• Apple TV simulators: tap for Select, swipe to move around, and use Back, TV, Play/Pause and Volume in the bar at the bottom.
• When your Mac has a default agent (set in Settings ▸ Agents on the Mac), a Group's + menu, its tabs' menus and an empty Group offer it too: New Claude Code Tab, or the one picked there, starts it straight away in the Group's folder.
• Editors follow their file when it's renamed or moved on your Mac, instead of saying it's missing, and Rename… in an editor's More menu renames it.
• Settings ▸ Hammock Documentation opens Hammock's new documentation, with a guide to the iPhone app.

Fixed
• Opening a session straight after going back from one no longer leaves the tab bar showing over it.
• Swiping right on a scheduled task for Run Now uses your theme's colour.
• The first message in a new conversation no longer blinks out and back as it's sent.
