New
• Agent view: a run of tool calls says what it did, such as “Read 4 files · ran 2 commands”, and how many failed.
• Agent view: an edit's diff names its file, numbers its lines, and has a button to copy it.
• Agent view: commands complete from any part of their name, and typing @ offers files from your project.
• Agent view: Latest says how many new messages came while you were reading further up, with a dot when the agent finishes or asks you something.
• Agent View: rebuilt to be much faster, so even long conversations open quickly and scroll smoothly.
• Agent view: tap the context gauge to see how many tokens are left, or to compact or clear the conversation.
• Agent view: with a hardware keyboard, press ↑ in an empty message box to bring back what you sent before.
• Agent view: with a hardware keyboard, press 1–9 to pick an answer to a prompt, ← and → to move between questions, and Esc to switch to the terminal.
• Connection status: every screen now says when Hammock is connecting to your computer or can't reach it, with Try Again, and you can pull down on Sessions or Groups to refresh.
• Copy from the terminal: touch and hold to select text, drag the handles to select more, then tap Copy.
• Files: a tab of your Mac's files as a tree; tap a file to open it beside the tree, or touch and hold to rename or move it.
• Haptics: feel a tap when you allow or deny a request, let someone join, approve a Hammock Key request or scan a pairing code.
• iPad: diff panes can show the old and new side by side when there's room.
• Key bar: a sticky ⌥ key, with symbols, Home and End a swipe away; ⌥ and a letter on a hardware keyboard send Esc and the letter.
• Live Activities: turn them on in Settings to follow your agents while you're away from your Mac, and Allow or Deny a permission without opening Hammock.
• Needs You widget: see what's waiting on you on your Home Screen or Lock Screen, and tap one to open it.
• Allow or Deny an agent's permission request right from its notification.
• Notifications name the agent, as on the Mac ("Claude finished"), and Hammock's icon counts what's waiting on you.
• Obsidian notes: the editor's Markdown preview now shows [[links]], embedded images and notes, callouts, highlights and tags as Obsidian does.
• Pinch a terminal to change the font size of every terminal.
• Run Again: when a terminal's program fails, a bar along its bottom starts it again in the same pane, and an agent's pane resumes its session.
• Screen sharing: minimize a Mac's screen to a bar that stays connected while you use the rest of Hammock, and tap it to have the screen back.
• Touch and hold a session or tab to see its screen, live, with Allow and Deny there for an agent asking permission.
